This notice explains what the survey stores about you while you take part in the study
"[study title]", why, and for how long.

## Who is responsible

[Name and address of the institution], represented by [legal representative]. For questions about
the study, contact [study team, e-mail address].

The data protection officer is [name, address, e-mail address].

## What the survey stores

- **Your consent**, and when you gave it.
- **Your answers** to the questionnaires, with the time you opened and submitted each step, and
  drafts of answers you have not submitted yet.
- **Which material you opened**, such as a tab or a file of an exercise, and when.
- **A pseudonymous rater ID** (such as R3). Your personal link is the only way in; the survey
  stores a one-way digest of it, not the link itself. Your name and e-mail address are not stored
  in the survey: only the study team knows which ID belongs to you.

The survey sets one cookie, which keeps you signed in after you open your personal link. It is
strictly necessary for the survey to work (section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG) and is deleted when you close
the browser. The survey uses no tracking, no analytics, and no content from other servers.

[The web server keeps no access logs. / The web server logs each request's time, address and IP
address for [n] days to detect attacks.]

## Why, and on which legal basis

Your answers are used to evaluate [the purpose of the study] and are processed on the basis of your
consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). Running the survey securely, including the cookie, is based on
[Article 6(1)(e) GDPR in conjunction with the applicable national provision].

## Who receives the data

Only the study team. The survey runs on servers of [institution]; no data leaves [country / the
European Union]. Results are published only in aggregate, and free-text answers only after any
detail that could identify you is removed.

## How long the data is kept

The survey's database is deleted by [date], after the answers are exported for analysis. The export
holds only your pseudonymous ID, and the link between the ID and your name is deleted by [date].
[The pseudonymous export is kept for [period] according to [research data policy].]

## Your rights

You may withdraw your consent at any time without giving reasons, by writing to [e-mail address];
your answers are then deleted. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it. You have the right
to access, rectify, erase, and restrict the processing of your data, and to object to it (Articles
15 to 21 GDPR). You may lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, for [institution] the
[name and address of the supervisory authority].
