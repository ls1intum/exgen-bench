Learning objective: students keep the state of several objects consistent across method calls and
signal invalid operations with exceptions.

Target concept: classes with collections as fields, and IllegalStateException for invalid state
changes.

Course phase: middle.

Scenario: a small library lends books to members and takes them back. A book cannot be lent twice or
returned when it is not on loan, and members can ask which books they currently have.

Constraints: the book class is given; two or three tasks; use java.util collections, no streams.

Prerequisites the students already have: classes with fields and methods, conditionals, loops,
and lists. Not covered yet: streams, inheritance, interfaces.

Expected effort: 30 to 45 minutes.
