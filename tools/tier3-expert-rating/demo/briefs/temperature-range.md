Learning objective: students write a boolean check with comparisons and build a short output text.
Target concept: conditions on doubles and string concatenation.
Target level: first-semester students after 3 weeks of Java.
Course phase: early.
Scenario: a room sensor has a comfortable temperature range; a program checks whether a reading lies
in the range and prints a one-line description of the range.
Constraints: one class, one or two tasks, at most six tests.
