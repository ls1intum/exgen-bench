Learning objective: students use an interface and inheritance so that a collection can treat
different shapes uniformly.

Target concept: interfaces, subclassing and polymorphic method calls.

Course phase: late.

Scenario: circles, rectangles and squares all report their area and perimeter, and a collection of
shapes adds up the area of everything it contains.

Constraints: a square must be modelled as a special rectangle; include a class diagram; three tasks.

Prerequisites the students already have: classes, constructors, lists, and loops. Not covered yet:
generic types of their own, lambdas, streams.

Expected effort: 30 to 45 minutes.
