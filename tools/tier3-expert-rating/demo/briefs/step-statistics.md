Learning objective: students traverse an array with a loop and compute simple statistics from it.

Target concept: array traversal with counters, accumulators and a running streak.

Course phase: early.

Scenario: a fitness tracker stores the step count of each day in an array, and the user has a daily
step goal. Students compute the average number of steps, the number of days that reach the goal,
and the longest run of consecutive days that reach the goal.

Constraints: plain loops only, no streams or library helpers; about three small tasks.

Prerequisites the students already have: variables, primitive types, conditionals, and loops. Not
covered yet: collections, streams, writing classes of their own.

Expected effort: 20 to 30 minutes.
