# Provider marks

Alibaba Cloud, Anthropic, DeepSeek, and Meta use paths from
[Simple Icons 16.21.0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/releases/tag/16.21.0). Google
Gemini and Mistral AI use the colored artwork from
[Lobe Icons Static SVG 1.94.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg/v/1.94.0).
Simple Icons is released under CC0 1.0; Lobe Icons is released under the MIT License.

Brand colors follow the corresponding public identities: Alibaba Cloud `#FF6A00`, Anthropic
`#141413`, DeepSeek `#4D6BFE`, Google Gemini `#3186FF` as the base of its multicolor mark, Meta
`#0467DF`, and Mistral AI `#FA520F` as the base of its multicolor mark.

Brand rights may still apply. Review the
[Simple Icons disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/16.21.0/DISCLAIMER.md),
the [Lobe Icons license](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons/blob/main/LICENSE), and the relevant
brand guidelines before reusing a mark. The marks identify model providers; they do not imply
endorsement.
